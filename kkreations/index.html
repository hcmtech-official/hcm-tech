Our story

Baked with a little home-kitchen stubbornness

KKreations World started the way most good things do — friends asking for "just one more" grazing box for their next party. What began on Instagram is now a full spread of custom cakes, cupcake bouquets, strawberry towers and grazing boxes, each one built by hand for the date on your calendar.

Every order is made fresh in small batches, so what you see is genuinely what you get — no factory shortcuts, no filler ingredients.

Questions

Before you enquire

How far in advance should I order?+
Simple items like cupcake bouquets or grazing boxes need about 1–2 weeks' notice. Tiered or heavily detailed number cakes and towers need 4 or more weeks, especially around peak periods.
Do I need to pay a deposit?+
Yes — a deposit confirms your date on the calendar. The remaining balance is due on pickup or delivery.
Do you deliver?+
Yes, delivery is available across Sydney for a fee based on distance. Pickup is also available by appointment.
Do you cater for dietary needs?+
Veg options are available across the menu — let us know your requirements when you submit your order.
