A Heritage of 50 Years

At Nosh India, our sweets are not just food; they are a legacy. Every single piece is made from an authentic, cherished 50-year-old family recipe passed down through generations.

Every batch is crafted with patience, love, and strict attention to traditional techniques—using pure khoya and slow-simmered rose and cardamom syrup. Everyone who has tasted our sweets at home has been blown away by the authentic flavor.

Now, for the first time, we are sharing this legendary taste with our community. Experience the warmth and love of a home-cooked Indian celebration.
