Explore Katoomba

The Carrington Hotel

A grand historic hotel opened in 1883. Known for its Victorian elegance, stained glass, and opulent public spaces.

How to get there: A short 2-minute walk from Katoomba Station exit.

Three Sisters & Echo Point

The iconic sandstone rock formation. Echo Point offers the best views of the valley and access to walking tracks.

Bus 686 Catch the loop bus from Katoomba St (outside Carrington).

Scenic World

Experience the world's steepest railway, the Scenic Skyway, and the Cableway over the Jamison Valley.

Bus 686 Connects directly from Echo Point or the Station.

Katoomba Street Shops

The heart of the town, filled with art deco buildings, vintage boutiques, art galleries, and cozy cafes.

How to get there: Right at the doorstep of Katoomba Station.

10:05 AM

11:45 AM

Arrive at Katoomba Station. Walk to the Carrington Hotel to admire the grand architecture and gardens.

Explore Katoomba Street and grab lunch at a local cafe (e.g., The Paragon or a cozy bakery).

Catch Bus 686 (Blue Mountains Bus Co) to Echo Point to see the Three Sisters.

Take Bus 686 to Scenic World. Ride the Scenic Railway and Skyway for breathtaking views.
